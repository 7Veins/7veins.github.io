About us

A sourcing desk for small brands that want to produce in Bangladesh.

matches small knitwear and apparel brands with vetted Bangladeshi factories, builds the cost sheet, and follows every sample through to shipment.

How we work

Three rules we do not bend.

Costing you can check

Every quote is broken into lines, so you can compare factories on yarn, knitting, finishing, trims and packing, not on one number.

Money goes factory-direct

You pay the factory yourself, by T/T or L/C. We never hold your funds, and our commission is paid by the factory.

Reports, not promises

Weekly sample and production updates with photos and measurements checked against your spec.

Who we work with

Small brands with a launch date and a target price.

We work with brands ordering to pcs per style in knitwear, cotton basics and sweaters, and that have no sourcing team of their own. We are based in Bangladesh and work with brands anywhere.
